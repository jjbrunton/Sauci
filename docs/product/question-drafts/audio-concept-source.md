# Play-safe spicy audio concept source

Draft source for private, up-to-60-second audio responses. This is editorial input only and is not a live catalogue import.

1. Theme: Flirty communication | Intensity: 2
   Prompt: In a warm, playful tone, record the compliment from your partner that would make you smile all day.
   Intended outcome: Your partner hears the exact kind of praise that lands for you and can use it naturally in a future message.

2. Theme: Flirty communication | Intensity: 3
   Prompt: Record a voice note finishing this sentence: “When you look at me like that, I want you to know...”
   Intended outcome: Your partner receives a personal signal of attraction and can reply with what they notice in that moment too.

3. Theme: Flirty communication | Intensity: 3
   Prompt: Leave your partner a voice note with one bold thing you would love them to say quietly when you are alone together.
   Intended outcome: Your partner learns a specific phrase or mood that could make a private moment feel more charged.

4. Theme: Flirty communication | Intensity: 4
   Prompt: Record the invitation you would most like to hear when your partner wants to turn an ordinary evening into a close one.
   Intended outcome: Your partner gets a clear, consent-forward way to initiate and can try it on a night that feels right.

5. Theme: Flirty communication | Intensity: 4

6. Theme: Anticipation | Intensity: 2
